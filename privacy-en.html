Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026 · Applies to: Lügat (Android, package com.abdul.lugat)

In short: Lügat keeps your words and your progress on your phone only. There is no account, we run no server, and the app contains no analytics or advertising tools. It goes online only when you start an action, and then sends only the word in question.

1. Who is responsible?

Lügat is developed and published by Abdul Samet Polat ("we"). Questions: lugat@example.com.

2. What data is there, and where is it kept?

The following is stored only in the app's storage on your device and never reaches us:

  • Decks, words, meanings, example sentences, tags, pictures and notes.
  • Review history, statistics, streaks, badges, exam results.
  • Settings: theme, language, goals, reminder times, the automatic-backup folder.
  • API keys you enter for an AI provider (in Android's encrypted secure storage, backed by the Keystore).
  • The Lügat Pro purchase status ("active / not active").

The app opts out of Android's automatic cloud backup (Google account backup); your data leaves the device only when you make a backup yourself.

3. When does the app go online?

Only in the cases below, and only when you start them. Every connection is encrypted with HTTPS.

3a. Online word suggestions (free, can be switched off)

While you add a word, the word you typed and the language pair are sent to these services, and the meanings or example sentences they return are shown to you:

  • Google Translate dictionary (Google LLC)
  • Wiktionary (Wikimedia Foundation)
  • MyMemory (Translated S.r.l.)
  • Tatoeba (Tatoeba Association)

Nothing else is sent (no name, no device identifier, none of your decks). The services' own privacy policies apply. Suggestions can be switched off entirely under Settings → Adding words; while off, the app does not contact these services.

3b. AI suggestions (Lügat Pro, with your own key)

If you enter your own API key in Settings, the word and language pair are sent, when you ask, to the provider you chose: Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI, Google (Gemini), or an OpenAI-compatible server whose address you entered. Your key is stored only on your device and sent directly to that provider; it never passes through us. How the provider handles the data is governed by its own policy.

3c. The Google Play purchase

Lügat Pro is bought through Google Play Billing. Google processes your payment details; we see no payment information, name or email. The app receives from Google Play only whether this Google account owns Lügat Pro, and stores that on the device. Google's privacy policy applies.

3d. This website

The privacy-policy and terms links open this site in the device browser. The site is static and uses no cookies, analytics or JavaScript. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts (your browser sends Google your IP address for that request).

4. Features that never go online

Spaced repetition, all study modes, pulling words out of a text, read-aloud (the device's own engine), notifications (scheduled on the device), home-screen widgets, CSV / JSON / .lugat files and automatic backups all work entirely on the device.

5. Permissions

  • Notifications: requested only if you turn a reminder on.
  • Camera: only when you choose to take a photo for a word; the photo is copied into the app's folder.
  • Gallery: the system photo picker is used; the app does not get access to your whole gallery.
  • Folder access: the one folder you pick for automatic backups, with the permission Android's folder picker grants.
  • Vibration, internet, Google Play billing.

6. Shared and backup files

When you share a deck as a .lugat file or make a backup, who receives that file and where it is kept is under your control. Backup files do not contain the purchase status.

7. Children

Lügat is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children. It collects no personal data from anyone.

8. Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes all data on the device. Backup files you made and .lugat files you shared are yours to delete. We hold no data to delete.

9. Your rights

You do not need to file a request for the access, rectification, erasure or portability rights under GDPR and similar laws: the data is on your device; you can export it as a JSON backup from within the app and delete it from Settings. If you have a question anyway, write to lugat@example.com.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at this address with the date above. An update that changes how data is handled will also say so inside the app.
